Love Show

Arcade Cabinet

3331 Polys
Autodesk Maya
Substance Painter

We landed on this planet, but these aliens seem to hate us! Show them some love with our love guns! These will make them love us instead :D

Love Show is a fictional arcade game inspired by water gun carnival games. Rather than using water, the love gun is an IR emitter used to shoot aliens on screen.

This cabinet was created for a 3D modeling class (the same one as SoundShower!). I created Love Show to add another cabinet to an arcade environment.

This model features a multitude of heart shapes, and I needed to balance the low-poly modeling with the curves of a heart.
