SMYRA

Arcade Cabinet

1276 Polys
Autodesk Maya
Substance Painter

Help the Monument! This pyramid grows with every successful hit! We want to take over the world, so help us grow!

SMYRA is a fictional rhythm arcade game inspired by Pump It Up and Dance Dance Revolution. Players are encouraged to play with a partner or by themselves. With 5 buttons per game pad, the possibilities of dancing is exponential!

This cabinet was created for a 3D modeling class. SMYRA is the 3rd arcade cabinet I created for an arcade environment that features both SoundShower and Love Show!

With a pyramid theme, I incorporated a lot of triangular shapes and a brick texture in the design. I also made sure to add plenty of speakers to allow players to follow the music.
