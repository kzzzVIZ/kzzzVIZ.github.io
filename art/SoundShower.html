SoundShower Arcade Cabinet

9503 Polys
Autodesk Maya
Substance Painter

Protect Our Planet! Our shield can't keep up its regeneration, so use the buttons to catch the stars! Punch The Meteors! Swipe on the arrow pads to punch away the meteors! Please don't punch the screen! We can't get that fixed!

SoundShower is a fictional arcade rhythm game with gameplay elements inspired by Project DIVA and MÚSECA. Gameplay is a lane-based system featuring four buttons and two touch pads.

I was very inspired by the physicality of Japanese arcade rhythm games, and I wanted this cabinet to have fun movements during gameplay.

This cabinet was created for a 3D modeling class. This was the first time I used both Autodesk Maya and Substance 3D Painter for 3D modeling.

For this model, I wanted to try working in a low-poly style. In retrospect, I can see some parts that can be more optimized, but I'm still proud of the work that I've done for this very first model.
