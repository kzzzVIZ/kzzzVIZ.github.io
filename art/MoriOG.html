I refuse to allow that foolish prince destroy the kingdom I was promised.

a hanging start is the story of Moriana Rosethorne. The prologue to the life she built from the ground up.

This model was created for my character modeling class. She was the first model I created using Maxon ZBrush.

Because this was my first character model, I was really nervous about texturing it. I wanted to utilize transparency in my textures, but I had some issues making it work in both Substance Painter and Arnold.
