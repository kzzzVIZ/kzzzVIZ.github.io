Sailor Pro Gear Slim

Spring Sky Colorway

9677 Polys
Autodesk Maya
Maxon Redshift

“In Spring the early morning Sky is the most beautiful sight! As the morning sunlight ‘creeps’ slowly over the hills, their outlines are colored a pinky red and wisps of purplish clouds trail over them.” - from sailor.co.jp

This model is inspired by the PROFESSIONALGEAR Slim Spring Sky Fountain Pen by Sailor. This model primarily uses procedural texturing using Redshift materials with a hand-drawn texture for the nib.

In recreating this pen, I took a lot of measurements, most of which were of the various diameters in the pen. By using circle curves with these diameters, I lofted the curves to fill out the space between them and create the mesh of the pen. I only needed a few adjustments to make it almost exactly the same pen I use every day.

I bought this pen after completing my first year in the Animation & Visualization program at UCF. Its colorway reminded me of a dress from the movie Barbie: A Fairy Secret. I really loved that movie, and seeing this pen reminded me of the joy I felt watching that movie and its visuals.
