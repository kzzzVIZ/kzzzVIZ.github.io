a hanging start

This is my thesis film for Animation & Visualization at UCF.

This story is the prologue to Moriana Rosethorne's life. What happened before she overcame her odds and became the queen through her own work. During this short, we discover her circumstances and the obstacles in her path to fulfilling the duty given to her as a child.

I absolutely loved modeling everything for this project. I can't wait to make even more in the future, whether in film or games.

Created using Autodesk Maya, Maxon ZBrush, and Substance 3D Painter. Edited in Adobe After Effects.

Donut

This is my version of the popular Blender Donut. Because the donut uses an adjustable primitive, I decided to turn it into a triangular seat for my character. I liked how it looked as a looping animation, moving up and down.

Bring Shadow to the PSP

This was created for an assignment for a computer art class with the goal of using Adobe Illustrator. This represents my love for Shadow the Hedgehog and how I wish I could've played his game on my beloved PlayStation Portable. I modified the PSP's colors to better match Shadow's coloring.
